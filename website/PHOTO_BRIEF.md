# Bean and Bear: flavour photography

**All 42 flavour photographs are in.** To replace one, save the new
image over the file of the same name in assets/img/flavours/. No code
changes are needed.

## The standard

Square, 1:1, JPEG, at least 1600 x 1600 px (the current photos are 1092
px). One deep-green Bean and Bear tub with a white rim, centred and
fully in frame, on marble with a warm blurred bar behind. Keep camera
height, lighting and tub identical across the set.

## The 42 flavours and their card text

The card text is the ingredient line shown on each card. For 1 to 20 it
is the text on the counter cards. For 21 to 42 it is written from each
recipe\'s own key ingredients in the recipe book.
