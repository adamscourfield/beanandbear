AI image tools often mis-set small print, so check any price at full
zoom. The safest fix is a board with no prices at all.

## Prompt A: blank board (recommended)

Use the original photo as the reference image.

> Edit the attached image. Keep the composition, lighting, people,
> plants, architecture and lettering exactly as they are. Change only
> the menu boards: each should be a plain deep-green board with a thin
> brass frame, the gold walking-bear mark and the single word GELATO in
> a fine gold serif at the top, and nothing else. No prices, no lists,
> no other text on any board.

## Prompt B: boards with the correct prices

> Edit the attached image. Keep the composition, lighting, people,
> plants, architecture and lettering exactly as they are. Change only
> the gelato menu board: a deep-green board with the gold walking-bear
> mark and GELATO at the top. Below it, in a clean gold serif, exactly

The website lays the logo and tagline over the store photograph, so the
photograph should have none of its own.

> with a warm, plant-filled interior, olive trees in stone planters, a
> small wooden bench and a green A-board. The fascia is completely
