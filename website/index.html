Our family

A family story,
told through gelato.

Premium in craft. Warm in character.

Bean & Bear began with making ice cream at home. In a busy family life, asking the children if they wanted to make ice cream had an unusual effect: everything else stopped. They came into the kitchen. We talked, shared things, laughed, messed around and made something together.

We have always called our children either the Bean or the Bear. That is where the name came from.

The deeper philosophy is simple. Family has brought extraordinary joy, meaning and resilience to life. Bean & Bear celebrates those small moments through flavours connected to people, places, holidays, traditions and family stories.
